Present Simple

Usamos para rotinas e hábitos — coisas que fazemos sempre.

✅ Affirmative (frases positivas)

I / You / We / They + verb   |   He / She / It + verb + S
I wake up early. · They play football.
He wakes up early. · She plays the piano.
Com he / she / it, o verbo ganha um -s!
⚠️ Regra de ouro do -s Com he, she, it, quase todo verbo ganha -s. Alguns são especiais:
Ends in…AddExample
normal+ splay → plays · read → reads
-o, -ch, -sh, -ss, -x+ esgo → goes · watch → watches · wash → washes
consonant + yy → iesstudy → studies
have (special!)→ hasHe has lunch at school.

❌ Negative & ❓ Questions

Aqui entram os ajudantes do e does.

I / you / we / they → don't / Do…? he / she / it → doesn't / Does…?
NegativeQuestion
I / You / We / TheyI don't play chess.Do you play chess?
He / She / ItHe doesn't play chess.Does he play chess?
⚠️ Cuidado! Depois de doesn't e Does, o verbo volta ao normal, SEM o -s! O does já carrega o -s.
✅ He doesn't watch TV.   ❌ He doesn't watches TV.
✅ Does she go swimming?   ❌ Does she goes…?
Affirmative → Negative → Question
She watches TV. → She doesn't watch TV. → Does she watch TV?

In · On · At

As três preposições de tempo. Cada uma tem seu lugar!

PrepositionUse for…Examples
INparts of the day, months, yearsin the morning · in the evening · in March
ONdays of the week & dateson Monday · on Saturdays · on weekends
ATexact times (horas) & nightat 6:30 · at 10 o'clock · at night
🎯 Truque para lembrar AT = horas certinhas (relógio) · ON = dias · IN = períodos maiores (manhã, mês, ano).
⭐ Exceções especiais at night (à noite) e at the weekend / on the weekend — os dois aparecem!
He wakes up at 6:30 in the morning, and on Sundays he stays home.

Telling the Time

Como dizer as horas em inglês.

🕐 Read the clock

Diga a hora e depois os minutos: hour + minutes.

3:00
It's three o'clock.
9:05
It's nine oh five.
7:35
It's seven thirty-five.
8:30
It's eight thirty.
12:00
It's midday / midnight.
6:15
It's six fifteen.
💡 Boa saber
• o'clock = hora exata (só use com :00) → 5:00 = five o'clock
• oh = para o zero → 9:05 = nine oh five
• a.m. = manhã (0h–12h) · p.m. = tarde/noite (12h–24h)
• midday = meio-dia (12 p.m.) · midnight = meia-noite (12 a.m.)

📝 Antes da prova — checklist

  • Sei os verbos de rotina e as atividades de tempo livre.
  • Coloco o -s certinho com he / she / it (go → goes, watch → watches, have → has).
  • Sei fazer frases negativas (don't / doesn't) e perguntas (Do / Does).
  • Lembro que depois de doesn't e Does o verbo fica sem -s.
  • Uso in / on / at corretamente.
  • Sei dizer as horas (o'clock, oh, a.m./p.m., midday/midnight).
  • Escrevo os dias da semana com letra maiúscula.
