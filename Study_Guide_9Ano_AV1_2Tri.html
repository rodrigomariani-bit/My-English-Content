02

Present Perfect Continuous

Para ações que começaram no passado e continuam agora (ou acabaram de parar), enfatizando a duração.

🔧 Structure

subject + have / has + been + verb-ing
I / you / we / they → have been he / she / it → has been
I have been training since 8 o'clock.
Eu estou treinando desde as 8h (e ainda estou).
She has been running every morning since March.
Ela corre toda manhã desde março.
❓ How long have you been practising skateboarding?
Perguntas: (question word) + have/has + subject + been + -ing?

⏱️ When to use it — for & since

WordUseExample
fora period of time (duração)for almost a year · for two hours
sincea starting point (ponto de início)since 8 o'clock · since March · since Monday
✅ Use Present Perfect Continuous for: ações em progresso que continuam agora, com foco em quanto tempo (How long…?).
03

Continuous vs. Simple

Present Perfect Continuous vs. Present Perfect Simple — a diferença que a prova adora cobrar.

Present Perfect ContinuousPresent Perfect Simple
Formhave/has been + -inghave/has + past participle
Focusthe activity / durationthe result or a completed action
ExampleShe has been studying the rules.She has been a member for two years.
⚠️ Stative verbs (verbos de estado) Alguns verbos não costumam ir no contínuo: be, know, have (posse), like, want, believe.
✅ I have known her my whole life.   ❌ I have been knowing…
✅ I have never seen her so motivated.   ❌ I have never been seeing…
✅ She has been running since March, and she has been a member of the gym for two years.
"has been running" (ação em progresso) + "has been" (estado — verbo be, sem -ing).
04

Future Forms

Três formas de falar do futuro: will, going to e o Present Continuous. Cada uma tem seu uso!

FormUse it for…Example
willdecisão na hora, promessa, oferta, previsãoDon't worry, I 'll answer it.
going toplano / intenção & previsão com evidênciaI'm going to study for the exam.
Present Continuousarranjo / compromisso já marcado (com horário)I'm meeting my cousins on Saturday.
Ana: What are you doing tonight?   Bia: I'm going to study. I plan to review the whole unit.
"plan to" mostra intenção → going to.
Your phone is ringing! — Don't worry, I'll answer it.
Decisão feita na hora → will.
I'm meeting my cousins at the festival. We arranged it last week.
"arranged" (já combinado) → Present Continuous.
🔑 Quick rule Combinou/marcou antes → going to ou Present Continuous. Decidiu agora → will.
05

Contrast Linkers

Conectivos de contraste (ideias opostas): although, though, in spite of / despite, however, but.

LinkerFollowed by…Position & example
althougha full clause (subject + verb)Although it was raining, they went running.
thougha clause; also common at the endThe park was crowded. I had a great time, though.
in spite of / despitea noun or verb-ingIn spite of the expensive tickets, the festival was amazing.
howeverstarts a new sentence + commaParkour looks dangerous. However, it is quite safe.
butjoins two clauses in one sentenceOur team trained hard, but we lost the final.
⚠️ Cuidado com a estrutura!
• although / though + frase completa (sujeito + verbo)
• in spite of / despite + substantivo ou verbo-ing (NÃO uma frase completa)
• however começa uma frase nova, sempre com vírgula depois
✅ In spite of the rain… / In spite of being tired…   ❌ In spite of it was raining…
